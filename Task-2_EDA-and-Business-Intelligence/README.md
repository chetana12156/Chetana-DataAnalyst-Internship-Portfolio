# Customer Segmentation & Marketing Analytics – Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) & Business Intelligence

## Objective

The objective of this project was to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on customer marketing data to identify patterns, trends, and relationships that can support business decision-making and customer-focused marketing strategies.

---

## Dataset

Customer Personality Analysis dataset containing customer demographic information, purchasing behavior, campaign responses, and customer engagement metrics.

---

## Project Overview

Exploratory Data Analysis was conducted to understand customer behavior and spending patterns across various dimensions. The analysis focused on customer demographics, purchasing habits, product preferences, campaign effectiveness, and customer engagement.

The project aimed to transform raw data into meaningful insights that could help businesses improve customer targeting, retention, and marketing performance.

---

## Analysis Performed

### Customer Demographics Analysis

* Age distribution analysis
* Income distribution analysis
* Marital status analysis
* Household composition analysis

### Spending Behavior Analysis

* Total spending analysis
* Product category spending comparison
* High-value customer identification
* Spending pattern exploration

### Customer Engagement Analysis

* Purchase channel analysis
* Website visit behavior
* Campaign response analysis
* Customer recency evaluation

### Relationship Analysis

* Income vs Spending relationship
* Correlation analysis between key variables
* Customer segmentation exploration

---

## Key Insights

* Higher-income customers generally spend more across product categories.
* Wine and meat products contribute significantly to overall customer spending.
* Customers actively engage through web purchases and online channels.
* Campaign response rates indicate opportunities for targeted marketing improvements.
* Customer spending behavior varies across demographic groups.
* Customer segmentation helps identify distinct customer value groups.

---

## Business Intelligence Findings

* Income can be used as an indicator of purchasing power.
* Customer segmentation enables personalized marketing strategies.
* Product preference analysis supports inventory and marketing decisions.
* Campaign performance metrics provide insights into customer engagement effectiveness.
* Customer behavior patterns can help improve retention and conversion strategies.

## Outcome

The Exploratory Data Analysis successfully uncovered meaningful patterns in customer behavior, spending trends, and marketing performance. The insights generated from this analysis formed the foundation for interactive dashboard development and statistical validation in subsequent stages of the project.

The findings demonstrate how data-driven analysis can support strategic business decisions, improve customer understanding, and enhance marketing effectiveness.
