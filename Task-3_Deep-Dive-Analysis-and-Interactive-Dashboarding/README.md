# Customer Segmentation & Marketing Analytics Dashboard

## Objective

To analyze customer behavior, spending patterns, purchase channels, campaign responses, and complaint trends using data analytics and visualization techniques in order to derive meaningful business insights.

---

## Dataset

Customer Personality Analysis dataset from Kaggle.

---

## Project Tasks

* Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
* Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* KPI Development
* Customer Segmentation
* Dashboard Development in Tableau
* Business Insight Generation

---

## Data Preparation

* Removed missing values and duplicate records
* Converted date columns into proper datetime format
* Standardized inconsistent categorical values
* Prepared dataset for analysis and visualization

---

## Feature Engineering

* Created **Age** using Year_Birth
* Created **Total_Spending** by combining product spending columns
* Created **Children** from Kidhome and Teenhome
* Created **Customer Segment** based on spending behavior

---

## KPIs Created

* Total Customers
* Average Income
* Average Spending
* Campaign Response Rate
* Complaint Rate

---

## Dashboard Visualizations

The Tableau dashboard includes:

* KPI Cards
* Customer Segment Distribution
* Income vs Spending Scatter Plot
* Purchase Channel Analysis
* Customer Response Analysis
* Complaint Rate Analysis

---

## Key Insights

* Higher income customers tend to spend more overall
* Medium-value customers formed the largest customer segment
* Web purchases showed strong customer engagement
* Campaign response rates highlighted opportunities for targeted promotions
* Complaint rates were relatively low across customer groups

---

---

## Outcome

The project successfully transformed raw customer data into an interactive and analysis-ready business intelligence dashboard.

Data preprocessing techniques improved data quality and consistency, while feature engineering enabled deeper customer behavior analysis. Tableau dashboards and KPI visualizations helped identify customer spending trends, campaign performance, purchasing behavior, and customer satisfaction metrics.

The final dashboard provides meaningful insights that can support targeted marketing strategies, customer retention efforts, and business decision-making.
